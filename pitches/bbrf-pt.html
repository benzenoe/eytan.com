01 — The Investment Thesis

One fund.
Three reinforcing legs.

Each leg hedges and amplifies the others. The structural return is not selling the Bitcoin — it is the spread between Bitcoin's long-run appreciation and a low, regulated borrowing cost.

01
Reserve Asset · Never Sold

Long Bitcoin.

Bitcoin is the hardest monetary asset ever created. Its continued monetisation — absorbing gold's market cap and gaining a foothold in sovereign reserves — is credible over a ten-year horizon. Across sustained four-year cycles Bitcoin has historically appreciated 40–60% per annum. The principal basis risk is regulatory, not structural.

In this structure: The holding company assembles a Bitcoin reserve in regulated institutional custody (Sygnum, Anchorage, or institutional Ledn class). Multisig from inception. Never on exchange. Collateral for conservative euro borrowing, not a position to be managed.
02
Dollar Short · EUR Hedge

Short the dollar.

With ~$39 trillion of US federal debt and ~$1 trillion a quarter in interest, the long-run path of least resistance is currency debasement rather than collapse. Euro-denominated hard assets provide structural protection. Borrowing dollars at a low, regulated cost against a Bitcoin reserve converts that thesis into a financeable position.

In this structure: USD is borrowed at conservative LTV through regulated lending (~2–5% APR indicative), swapped to euros, and deployed to acquire Portuguese real estate. The borrowing cost is real and is included in all return illustrations.
03
Hard Asset · Yield Generating

Long Portugal.

Lisbon at €5–6K/m² remains well below Paris (€10–12K) and comparable to Barcelona, with mature tourism infrastructure, supply constrained by licensing, and durable inbound demand. Euro-denominated, income-producing, and a natural hedge against dollar debasement.

In this structure: Portuguese rental income services the borrowing cost; surplus flows to investors via the preferred dividend. Asset appreciation compounds alongside the Bitcoin reserve. Each acquisition closes into a dedicated Portuguese SPV (Lda.).

The structural alpha is in not selling the Bitcoin. The spread between Bitcoin's appreciation over a sustained cycle and a low, regulated borrowing cost is the trade. Portuguese rental income and asset appreciation pays the carry. The Luxembourg structure makes every step transparent and auditable enough for institutional investors to underwrite.

03 — The Structure

A corporate structure.
Not a token scheme.

Investors hold a senior, familiar security — convertible preferred shares of a Luxembourg holding company. The on-chain layer is a transparency and identity feature, not the legal interest. Capital and governance sit in the holding company; operations execute via the Portuguese subsidiaries.

Issuer · Luxembourg

SOPARFI Holding Company

Issues the convertible preferred shares. Holds the capital raised. Holds the Bitcoin reserve. Owns the Portuguese operating company and per-asset SPVs. Benefits from Luxembourg's participation-exemption regime and EU directives.

Reserve Asset

Bitcoin Reserve

Held in regulated institutional custody. Multisig from inception. Never on exchange. Collateral for conservative dollar and euro borrowing. The Bitcoin is never sold — it compounds untouched.

Operations · Portugal

Portuguese Operating Company

Sources, executes, and manages acquisitions under mandate. Coordinates investor reporting, NAV inputs, and capital deployment. One Sociedade Unipessoal, Lda. per asset for clean liability ring-fencing.

Per-Asset SPVs

Property SPVs (Lda.)

One Portuguese SPV per acquisition. Clean liability ring-fencing and disposal optionality. Each SPV receives borrowed euros to fund the acquisition; rental income flows up to the holdco.

Transparency Layer

bbrf-pt.eth · On-Chain

Tokenised share register, published governance and acquisition records, and the bbrf-pt.eth identity on ENS. A transparency and reporting feature — it does not alter the legal rights of the convertible preferred shares.

Independent Services

Professional Bench

Legal counsel, fund administration, regulated custody, smart-contract audit, and independent valuation — external and bound by mandate, not ownership. Five functions permanently independent of the operating company.

04b — A Deal, End to End

From capital in
to yield out.

Every acquisition follows the same seven steps. Every step is documented. Every decision is published to investors and recorded via the bbrf-pt.eth identity.

How a deal flows through the structure

  1. Capital in. An eligible professional investor subscribes for convertible preferred shares. The share register (and its on-chain mirror via bbrf-pt.eth) records the holding.
  2. Reserve allocated. A defined portion of capital is allocated to the Bitcoin reserve, in regulated institutional custody. The Bitcoin is not sold.
  3. Conservative borrowing. Euros are borrowed against the reserve at a conservative LTV through regulated lending at ~2–5% APR.
  4. Advisory sources. A top-tier institutional real-estate advisor surfaces a deal and prepares a full underwriting dossier — location, net yield, financing terms, and exit scenarios.
  5. Investment Committee approves. The acquisition is approved by the Company's Investment Committee against defined criteria. An independent Portuguese valuation confirms pricing. The decision and its rationale are published to investors and recorded on-chain.
  6. Acquisition executes. The Portuguese operating company closes the asset into a dedicated SPV (Lda.).
  7. Yield flows. Net rental income is distributed periodically to investors, after the preferred dividend and costs, in EUR or EURC.
