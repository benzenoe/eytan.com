Payment Terms & Protections

  • Invoices issued on the 1st of each month; due within 14 calendar days
  • Deemed approval: milestones considered accepted after 7 business days without written feedback
  • Late payment: 1.5% per month interest on overdue balances after 30-day grace period
  • Termination: 30 days written notice; work in progress billed pro-rata to termination date
  • IP transfer: all deliverables transfer to Ashth upon full payment of the relevant month
  • Ad spend: paid directly by Ashth to platforms (Google, Meta, TikTok, Amazon) — never routed through agency
  • Currency: all fees in EUR at invoice-date mid-market exchange rate; FX fees borne by payer
