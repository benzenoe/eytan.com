Payment Terms & Protections

  • Invoices issued on the 1st of each month; due within 14 calendar days
  • Deemed approval: milestones are considered approved after 7 business days without written feedback
  • Late payment: 1.5% monthly interest on overdue balances after 30 days
  • Termination: 30 days written notice; work in progress billed pro-rata to termination date
  • IP transfer: all deliverables transfer to Ashth upon full payment of the relevant phase
  • Currency: all fees in EUR; FX conversions at invoice-date mid-market rate
