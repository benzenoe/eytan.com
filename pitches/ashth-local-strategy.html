The Approach

Three Channels. One Coherent Strategy.

Before investing in large-scale digital campaigns, Ashth Europe establishes real market presence through three complementary channels — exclusive private events, a weekly interim showroom in key cities, and white-glove home appointments — all driven by targeted digital marketing on LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram.

Channel 1

Private Showcase Events

Curated cocktail evenings in Luxembourg. By invitation only. 20–30 guests from the professional and financial community. The Ashth Cut® as the centrepiece conversation.

  • Intimate, exclusive format — luxury is experienced, not browsed
  • Pre-qualified guest list from professional networks
  • Direct sales at the event + appointment bookings for follow-up
  • Builds brand reputation and local client network in Luxembourg
  • Each event funds the next through direct sales
Channel 2

Concierge Appointments

Clients browse the online catalogue, select pieces of interest, and book a private in-person appointment. Kuntal brings a curated selection directly to the client — at their home, office, or chosen venue.

  • Website acts as a catalogue + appointment booking engine
  • Client qualifies themselves by selecting interest & budget
  • Appointments within 300km radius of Luxembourg
  • Outer-radius appointments (incl. Paris) require minimum sale threshold
  • White-glove service as a brand differentiator
Channel 3

Weekly Interim Showroom

One dedicated appointment day per week at a shared professional office in Luxembourg, Brussels, and Paris on rotation. Clients come to a proper showroom environment — not a home, not a café. Builds the perception of a permanent European presence without a lease.

Why This Approach — Not Digital Advertising First

Luxury jewelry at the €2,000–20,000 price point is not impulse-purchased through Instagram ads. It is bought through trust, personal experience, and touch. European luxury brands — from Cartier to Van Cleef — have always led with personal relationships before scaling to mass media. This strategy builds that trust base first. Digital marketing amplifies what already exists; it cannot create it from scratch.

Running the Perfect Showcase Evening

Every detail of the event is intentional. Exclusivity is the product. The moment a guest picks up the Ashth Cut® and looks through a loupe, the sale begins.

The Ashth Cut® Moment

This is the anchor of every event. Place the Ashth Cut® under a small loupe or magnifier on its own stand. Every guest who looks through it will stop and stare — 97 facets, the hidden diamond inside the diamond. That reaction is your best sales tool.

Men's Selection

Bring 4–5 pieces specifically for men — bracelets, rings, and cufflinks if available. A room of bankers and lawyers will include men buying for themselves or their partners. Men's jewellery is under-served in this market and commands strong emotional purchase decisions.

After the Event

Every guest who expressed interest but didn't buy gets a WhatsApp message within 24 hours — a photo of the piece they admired, the price, and a payment link or appointment booking link. WhatsApp has a 98% open rate. This is how the family closes in India. Replicate it in Europe.

Channel 2 — Appointment Playbook

Running the Private Appointment

The home or office appointment is the highest-conversion format in luxury sales. The client is in their own space, relaxed, and has already self-qualified through the booking form. Kuntal's job is to match the right piece to the right person.

📍

Where to Meet

  • ✓ Best
    Client's home — highest comfort, highest conversion. They're in their own space, partners are present, the environment invites purchase.
  • ✓ Good
    Client's office — fast, professional, effective for gifts and milestone purchases.
  • ✓
    Interim showroom / day office — neutral, professional, great for clients who prefer not to share their home address.
  • ✓
    Hotel lobby lounge — comfortable, neutral, good for first-time clients.
  • ✗ Avoid
    Cafés or casual venues — undersells the product and the brand. The setting is part of the luxury.
📋

The Booking Form — Your Qualification Filter

The form does the qualification work before Kuntal commits to a drive. Every field is intentional.

  • → Name + location (city + country)
  • → Which collection or piece caught their attention
  • → Occasion — engagement, gift, anniversary, personal
  • → Budget range — €500–2K / €2K–5K / €5K–15K / €15K+ (the key qualifier)
  • → Preferred date window + WhatsApp number

The Budget Rule

€500–2K → directed to the website shop for self-service purchase.
€2K+ → confirmed appointment, Kuntal responds within 24hrs.

Recommended Tool: Calendly (€15/month)

Connect a Calendly booking form to Kuntal's calendar. Embed on ashth.eu — on every product page, the homepage, and the navigation. No custom coding required. Can be live within one day. Clients select a time slot, fill in the qualification questions, and Kuntal reviews before confirming. Simple, professional, and scalable.

Revenue Context

At a conservative 20% margin, a single sale of a €5,000 piece generates €1,000 net. One €15,000 piece sale covers 2–3 months of the entire marketing budget. The goal of Phase 1 is not to generate scale — it is to validate the European market, build the first client base, and create the proof of concept that justifies Phase 2 investment.

Roadmap

Phase 1 — 6 Month Plan

A deliberate, month-by-month build. Infrastructure first. Audience second. Events third. Appointments run in parallel from Month 2 onward.

Month 1

Foundation

LinkedIn company page and Kuntal's profile live. Facebook Business page launched. "Book a Private Viewing" added to ashth.eu. First event venue confirmed for Month 2. Guest list building begins. Indian expat community outreach in Luxembourg.

Month 2

First Luxembourg Event

First cocktail showcase in Luxembourg City. 20–30 invited guests from the professional and financial community. Direct sales target: 2–4 pieces. First appointment bookings from the website go live. LinkedIn and Facebook ads launched at modest budget.

Month 3

Appointments + Optimization

First private appointment week across Luxembourg region. Event follow-ups converted to appointments. Digital ad performance reviewed and optimized. Paris visit day scheduled. Second Luxembourg event guest list begins.

Month 4

Regional Expansion

First appointments confirmed beyond the 1-hour radius — Brussels, Frankfurt, or Paris depending on pipeline. Outer-radius appointments are batched where possible. LinkedIn and Facebook ads extended to cover the broader regional geography. Distance-based qualification filter active on all booking requests.

Month 5

Established Rhythm

Regular Luxembourg events + a consistent regional appointment pipeline. Outer-radius visits scheduled when qualified leads justify the drive. Client referral network beginning to generate organic introductions across the region.

Month 6

Phase 1 Review + Phase 2 Planning

Sales data, client database, and digital performance reviewed. Assessment of whether Phase 2 — broader EU digital campaign — is warranted based on demonstrated demand. Decision on expanding inventory, adding markets, or deepening Luxembourg and Paris.
