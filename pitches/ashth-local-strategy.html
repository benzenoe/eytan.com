The Approach

Two Channels. One Coherent Strategy.

Before investing in large-scale digital campaigns, Ashth Europe establishes real market presence through two proven luxury sales channels — exclusive private events and concierge home appointments — powered by targeted digital marketing on LinkedIn and Facebook.

Channel 1

Private Showcase Events

Curated cocktail evenings in Luxembourg and Paris. By invitation only. 20–30 guests from the professional and financial community. The Ashth Cut® as the centrepiece conversation.

  • Intimate, exclusive format — luxury is experienced, not browsed
  • Pre-qualified guest list from professional networks
  • Direct sales at the event + appointment bookings for follow-up
  • Builds brand reputation in both cities simultaneously
  • Each event funds the next through direct sales
Channel 2

Concierge Appointments

Clients browse the online catalogue, select pieces of interest, and book a private in-person appointment. Kuntal brings a curated selection directly to the client — at their home, office, or chosen venue.

  • Website acts as a catalogue + appointment booking engine
  • Client qualifies themselves by selecting interest & budget
  • Appointments within 300km radius of Luxembourg
  • Paris appointments scheduled on dedicated visit days
  • White-glove service as a brand differentiator

Why This Approach — Not Digital Advertising First

Luxury jewelry at the €2,000–20,000 price point is not impulse-purchased through Instagram ads. It is bought through trust, personal experience, and touch. European luxury brands — from Cartier to Van Cleef — have always led with personal relationships before scaling to mass media. This strategy builds that trust base first. Digital marketing amplifies what already exists; it cannot create it from scratch.

The Indian Expat Audience — Fastest Entry Point

Luxembourg and Paris together host one of Europe's most significant Indian professional communities — EU institution employees, finance executives, technology leaders. Appointment System

The Website as a Concierge Platform

The existing ashth.eu catalogue requires one addition: a "Book a Private Viewing" flow. Clients browse the collection, identify pieces of interest, and request a personal appointment. Kuntal brings the jewellery to them.

🖥️

Current Website Role

The ashth.eu catalogue showcases the collection beautifully — product photography, collection descriptions, the Ashth Cut® story, pricing in euros. This stays exactly as-is. The only addition is a booking entry point on every product page and the homepage.

📅

New: "Book a Private Viewing"

A single button — on every product page, on the homepage, in the navigation — opens a short qualifying form. The client tells us what they want to see and where they are. Kuntal reviews and confirms within 24 hours.

Luxembourg Appointments

Available any business day. Client's home, office, or a neutral venue of their choice. Within 90 minutes of Luxembourg City. Same-week availability for serious enquiries.

🗼

Paris Appointments

Dedicated Paris visit days — once or twice per month. Multiple appointments batched on the same day. Clients in Paris are contacted when a visit is scheduled and offered a confirmed slot.

Client Qualification

Not every enquiry becomes an appointment. The form's budget field and occasion context allow Kuntal to prioritise serious buyers. Lower-budget enquiries are directed to the online catalogue for self-service purchase.

Revenue Context

At a conservative 20% margin, a single sale of a €5,000 piece generates €1,000 net. One €15,000 piece sale covers 2–3 months of the entire marketing budget. The goal of Phase 1 is not to generate scale — it is to validate the European market, build the first client base, and create the proof of concept that justifies Phase 2 investment.

Roadmap

Phase 1 — 6 Month Plan

A deliberate, month-by-month build. Infrastructure first. Audience second. Events third. Appointments run in parallel from Month 2 onward.

Month 1

Foundation

LinkedIn company page and Kuntal's profile live. Facebook Business page launched. "Book a Private Viewing" added to ashth.eu. First event venue confirmed for Month 2. Guest list building begins. Indian expat community outreach in Luxembourg.

Month 2

First Luxembourg Event

First cocktail showcase in Luxembourg City. 20–30 invited guests from the professional and financial community. Direct sales target: 2–4 pieces. First appointment bookings from the website go live. LinkedIn and Facebook ads launched at modest budget.

Month 3

Appointments + Optimization

First private appointment week across Luxembourg region. Event follow-ups converted to appointments. Digital ad performance reviewed and optimized. Paris visit day scheduled. Second Luxembourg event guest list begins.

Month 4

Paris Launch

First Paris event — Indian diaspora community and luxury lifestyle audience. 2–3 appointment days in Paris batched together. LinkedIn ads expanded to Paris job titles and locations. Facebook event boosted to Paris luxury interests audience.

Month 5

Dual-City Rhythm

One Luxembourg event + one Paris visit day per month becomes the standard cadence. Appointment pipeline running consistently from website. Client referral network beginning to generate organic introductions.

Month 6

Phase 1 Review + Phase 2 Planning

Sales data, client database, and digital performance reviewed. Assessment of whether Phase 2 — broader EU digital campaign — is warranted based on demonstrated demand. Decision on expanding inventory, adding markets, or deepening Luxembourg and Paris.
