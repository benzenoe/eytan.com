01 — The Three Pillars

Agents who own.
Knowledge that compounds.
Technology that cannot lie.

The Association is built around three groups in order of priority. Culture defines the agent community and its ethos. Education gives members the market and blockchain knowledge to be effective. Technology — the DAO, the MLS, the vault — is the tool that makes it all work. In that order.

01
Culture · The Agent Community

Agents who are owners, not dues-payers.

The real estate industry has a culture problem. Agents pay hundreds of dollars a year to associations that serve institutions, not practitioners. RealMLS.eth inverts that. Members hold equity in the assets they help source. The culture of the association is ownership — skin in the game, aligned incentives, shared upside.

In practice: A professional community where listing transparency is on-chain, offers cannot go missing, commissions are visible to all parties, and every agent has a stake in the portfolio they are building. NAR could never offer this.
02
Education · Market & Blockchain Literacy

The most informed agents win the most deals.

Two bodies of knowledge that most RE professionals currently lack entirely: how Portugal's market actually works at the macro level, and how tokenised property ownership functions in practice. The Association's education layer closes both gaps — producing agents who understand both the trade and the technology well enough to bring clients in.

In practice: Mastermind sessions, deal underwriting workshops, blockchain-for-RE certification, and Portugal market intelligence. Agents who complete the programme understand why Bitcoin-collateralised property acquisition is not speculation — it is prudent capital management.
03
Technology · The MLS On-Chain

The listing database that cannot lie.

The traditional MLS is a black box. Offers disappear. Commissions are hidden. Data is locked behind association walls. A blockchain-native MLS changes every one of those conditions. Every listing is a token. Every offer is an on-chain record. Every transaction is immutable and visible to all parties. The technology does not replace the agent — it makes the agent trustworthy by default.

In practice: The DAO governs acquisition decisions. The TTF tokenises each property SPV. The BTC/USD vault funds acquisitions without selling the treasury. The DeFi borrowing facility replaces centralised lenders. All of it exists to serve the agents and investors who make up the community.

The association model draws on existing blockchain-native governance frameworks already operating in Portugal — contribution-based membership, democratic voting, and a culture-first hierarchy that keeps the community at the centre. RealMLS.eth is not starting from scratch.

09 — Legal Precedent

Not just a DAO.
A legal milestone.

RealMLS.eth has a genuine legal ambition: to be the first blockchain real estate association formally recognised under Portuguese and EU law — setting the precedent for how on-chain property institutions are structured across Europe.

Portugal · CMVM pathway

The CMVM (Comissão do Mercado de Valores Mobiliários) is a navigable regulator. The TTF and equity token structure need formal opinion, but Portugal has shown willingness to engage with blockchain entities. VdA and Morais Leitão have the relevant expertise.

Luxembourg · MiCA compliance

The holdco sits in Luxembourg — Eytan's home jurisdiction, MiCA-compliant, with the treaty network for Portugal RE investment. Luxembourg RAIF or Portuguese FIA are both viable structures for the RIT. Luxembourg gives institutional credibility to family office investors.

Associative law · Portugal

Blockchain-native associations have already navigated Portuguese associative law — written constitutions, ENS identities, contribution-based membership. The path is established. RealMLS.eth can follow it with confidence.

ENS identity · on-chain permanence

RealMLS.eth is hosted on ENS/IPFS — a fully on-chain identity. The ENS record is the permanent, uncensorable proof of the institution's existence, ungovernable by any single party and accessible forever.

10 — Open Questions

What we need
to resolve together.

Structural decisions that need to be resolved before legal formation begins. None are blockers at concept stage — but each has real implications for the capital raise and governance design.

01

Equity form — token or trust unit?

ERC-1400 security token / ERC-20 governance token / RIT trust units. The legal form determines CMVM requirements and the governance model. Needs legal opinion before proceeding.

02

RIT jurisdiction?

Portuguese FIA or Luxembourg RAIF. Luxembourg preferred given MiCA compliance and existing ties. Determines tax treatment and investor access rules for the capital raise.

03

What is TTF exactly?

Internal mechanism or licensed entity? CMVM review required to determine whether it constitutes a regulated securities issuance platform before the first tokenised deal.

04

BTC treasury domicile?

Luxembourg holdco (MiCA-friendly) or separate DAO multisig treasury with independent governance. Determines custody risk profile and institutional LP comfort level.

05

Is Advisory a separate licensed entity?

Must be clarified before CMVM filing. Separate from the SoldHere brokerage licence or operating under it? Defines the deal sourcing and underwriting legal structure.

06

Static or dynamic ratio engine?

Fixed equity allocation at time of entry vs. dynamic oracle-fed ratio adjusting with BTC/USD market conditions. Impacts fairness for later investors and governance complexity.
