01 — The Association Model

One DAO.
Full transparency.
No intermediaries.

The Association is built for investors. The DAO governs every acquisition decision. The technology makes every step transparent and verifiable.

01
Community · Investors

Capital partners with skin in the game.

The association is for investors who share conviction in the three-leg thesis — Bitcoin-native individuals, family offices, and crypto-native capital that wants hard asset exposure without selling the stack. Members are owners of the portfolio, not passive LPs. Governance rights, equity upside, and transparency by default.

The Advisory — an independent licensed entity appointed by the DAO — sources, underwrites, and presents every deal. The DAO votes. The Advisory executes. Every transaction is visible on-chain.
02
Intelligence · Deal Flow

The DAO needs to vote. Members need to know.

Every acquisition goes to a DAO vote. That means members need two bodies of knowledge most investors currently lack: how Portugal's real estate market works at the deal level, and how the BTC/USD/RE macro thesis translates into specific acquisition criteria. The intelligence layer turns passive capital into informed governance.

In practice: The Advisory brings fully underwritten deal packages to the DAO — location, net yield, tokenisation structure, BTC-collateralised financing terms, and exit scenario. Members vote. The best deals get funded.
03
Technology · The Protocol

Transparent by design. Ungovernable by any one party.

Every deal is on-chain. Every vote is recorded. Every ownership stake is verifiable. Every distribution is automatic. The technology replaces opacity with proof — no hidden terms, no manual settlement, no disputed records.

In practice: The Advisory presents a deal. The DAO votes. The TTF tokenises the property SPV. The BTC/USD vault funds the purchase without liquidating the treasury.

The association model draws on existing blockchain-native governance frameworks already operating in Portugal — contribution-based membership, democratic voting, and a culture-first hierarchy that keeps the community at the centre. RealMLS.eth is not starting from scratch.

03 — The Structure

Three tiers.
One governance.

The RealMLS.eth DAO governs a three-layer structure — with the Association sitting above everything as the cultural and community layer. The DAO is how the institution governs itself on-chain.

Layer 1

Capital & Equity

Qualified investors enter with a minimum of €50,000. The ratio engine allocates equity proportionally to capital committed. On-chain DAO shares issued via Ethereum — the membership stake in the institution. Retail access subject to CMVM regulatory clearance.

Layer 2 · RIT

Operations

Real Investment Trust. BTC treasury posts collateral. Vault borrows against BTC at market DeFi rates. Management Co deploys capital into acquisitions on DAO approval.

Layer 3

Assets

BTC in multisig custody. Portugal RE in individual SPVs. TTF tokenises each SPV and issues equity to members. The Advisory sources, underwrites, and executes every acquisition on behalf of the DAO.

10 — Legal Precedent

Not just a DAO.
A legal milestone.

RealMLS.eth has a genuine legal ambition: to be the first blockchain real estate association formally recognised under Portuguese and EU law — setting the precedent for how on-chain property institutions are structured across Europe.

Portugal · CMVM pathway

The CMVM (Comissão do Mercado de Valores Mobiliários) is a navigable regulator. The TTF and equity token structure need formal opinion, but Portugal has shown willingness to engage with blockchain entities. VdA and Morais Leitão have the relevant expertise.

Luxembourg · MiCA compliance

The holdco sits in Luxembourg — Eytan's home jurisdiction, MiCA-compliant, with the treaty network for Portugal RE investment. Luxembourg RAIF or Portuguese FIA are both viable structures for the RIT. Luxembourg gives institutional credibility to family office investors.

Associative law · Portugal

Blockchain-native associations have already navigated Portuguese associative law — written constitutions, ENS identities, contribution-based membership. The path is established. RealMLS.eth can follow it with confidence.

ENS identity · on-chain permanence

RealMLS.eth is hosted on ENS/IPFS — a fully on-chain identity. The ENS record is the on-chain identity of the institution — renewed annually, uncensorable, and ungovernable by any single party.

Vault funds it

€31,500/yr net rental yield (4.2% × €750K). Distributed quarterly via smart contract in EURC. Members receive proportional to their token holdings. On-chain, automatic, auditable.

11 — Open Questions

What we need
to resolve together.

Structural decisions that need to be resolved before legal formation begins. None are blockers at concept stage — but each has real implications for the capital raise and governance design.

01

Equity form — token or trust unit?

ERC-1400 security token / ERC-20 governance token / RIT trust units. The legal form determines CMVM requirements and the governance model. Needs legal opinion before proceeding.

02

RIT jurisdiction?

Portuguese FIA or Luxembourg RAIF. Luxembourg preferred given MiCA compliance and existing ties. Determines tax treatment and investor access rules for the capital raise.

03

What is TTF exactly?

Internal mechanism or licensed entity? CMVM review required to determine whether it constitutes a regulated securities issuance platform before the first tokenised deal.

04

BTC treasury domicile?

Luxembourg holdco (MiCA-friendly) or separate DAO multisig treasury with independent governance. Determines custody risk profile and institutional LP comfort level.

05

Advisory structure — legal scope

The Advisory entity holds a Portuguese brokerage licence and operates on behalf of the DAO. Clarify with CMVM whether deal advisory and brokerage execution can sit within the same entity, or require a separate legal wrapper for the underwriting function.

06

Static or dynamic ratio engine?

Fixed equity allocation at time of entry vs. dynamic oracle-fed ratio adjusting with BTC/USD market conditions. Impacts fairness for later investors and governance complexity.
