Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Eytan Benzeno ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use eytan.com and its associated blog publishing tools, including integrations with third-party platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and X (Twitter).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name and email address when you submit a contact form.
  • Usage data — browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site (via standard server logs).
  • Social media tokens — access tokens obtained through Facebook Login are used solely to publish blog content to pages and groups you administer. These tokens are stored securely on our backend and are never shared with third parties.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to contact form inquiries.
  • To publish blog posts to social media platforms on your behalf (admin use only).
  • To improve website performance and user experience.

3. Facebook Integration

This website uses the Facebook Graph API to publish blog content to Facebook Pages and Groups administered by the site owner. This functionality is used solely by the site administrator. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data from other Facebook users.

Access tokens obtained via Facebook Login are:

  • Used only to post content to pages and groups you own or administer.
  • Stored securely in environment variables on our backend server.
  • Never used to access your friends list, private messages, or personal profile data beyond what is required for publishing.
  • Revocable at any time via your Facebook App Settings.

4. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of any data we hold about you at any time by contacting us at eytan@benzeno.com. For Facebook data specifically, you can also revoke app access directly through your Facebook Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

This site uses the following third-party services:

  • Facebook Graph API — for social media publishing.
  • Tidio — for live chat support (see Tidio Privacy Policy).
  • OpenAI — for AI-generated content optimization (no personal data is sent).
  • Railway / Neon — for backend hosting and database storage.

6. Cookies

This site uses minimal session cookies for admin authentication only. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Third-party services (e.g., Tidio) may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption and secure server-side storage of API credentials. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
