The Venue

MOB-ART studio

Contemporary art gallery · Ville-Haute

A gallery in the heart of the old town

MOB-ART studio is a contemporary art gallery on Avenue de la Porte-Neuve, a short walk from Place d’Armes and Boulevard Royal. Founded in 2017 and run by gallerist Luc Schroeder, it shows living painters, sculptors, photographers and graphic designers in a bright, welcoming space. For one evening, ASHTH’s diamonds join the art on its walls.

By car

The Théâtre, Knuedler and Royal-Hamilius car parks are all within a few minutes’ walk and open around the clock.

By tram or bus

Public transport in Luxembourg is free. The Hamilius tram stop on Boulevard Royal is a short walk from the gallery.

Access

The gallery has a step-free entrance and is at street level on Avenue de la Porte-Neuve.
