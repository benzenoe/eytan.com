A Personal Welcome · Head Of European Operations

Meet Kuntal Shah

Kuntal Shah leads ASHTH in Europe from Luxembourg, as Managing Partner of Kaleidoscopic Splendor S.à r.l., the company behind ASHTH Europe.

She is the person our clients meet. Every private consultation, bespoke commission and appointment in Luxembourg is handled by her personally, from the first conversation to the moment a piece is placed in your hand.

A fourth-generation jeweller from a prominent diamond family, Kuntal grew up in the trade before training as a lawyer. She holds an LL.M. from American University and an LL.B. from the University of Wales, and built an international career in intellectual property and business law at firms including Jones Day and Crawford Bayley & Co., handling patent litigation, trademark registration and cross-border legal work in the United States, the United Kingdom and Asia.

ASHTH brings those two halves of her life together: the heritage of a diamond family, and a lawyer’s insistence on certification, provenance and doing things properly. Her brief is to bring ASHTH to Europe with the same care the house is known for: Luxembourg first, then Paris and Brussels.

Our Story

Born of the diamond trade. Made for the century ahead.

ASHTH was founded by diamond-industry veteran Mukesh Shah and co-founder Kaivan Shah, who bring decades of experience in diamond manufacturing and the international jewellery trade.

They saw a new generation asking more of the world’s most meaningful stone: the same fire and brilliance, with nothing taken from the earth to make it. Their answer was a house where science, craftsmanship and conscience meet.

Every ASHTH diamond is laboratory-grown, above ground and under controlled conditions. It shares the physical, chemical and optical properties of a mined diamond, and every one is certified.

Today our designs span engagement rings and solitaires, eternity bands, tennis bracelets, statement necklaces and pieces made for every day. Each is made to be worn for a lifetime and passed on.

Our Promise In Three Words

Every word in our tagline rests on something you can verify.

Luxury has long asked to be taken on trust. We would rather be checked. Here is what each of our three words means in practice, and the facts that stand behind it.

I

Sustainable

sus·tain·a·ble · adj. · Able to continue without depleting what it draws on.

Every ASHTH diamond is grown above ground, atom by atom, from a single seed crystal. There is no mine behind it, no excavated landscape, no displaced earth and no tailings. What remains is the diamond itself, as brilliant as any ever unearthed.

  • The proof
  • Grown above ground in a controlled environment, not extracted from a mine.
  • No open-pit excavation and no disturbed land behind any ASHTH stone.
  • Known origin on every diamond, documented in its certificate.
II

Sensible

sen·si·ble · adj. · Guided by sound judgement; the reasonable choice.

Our jewellery travels from our own factory directly to you. There are no wholesalers, no brokers and no layers of retail mark-up between the bench and your hand. The value stays where it belongs: in the stone, the setting and the craftsmanship.

  • The proof
  • Designed, cut, set and finished in-house, then shipped direct.
  • One short, accountable path from factory to client, with no intermediaries.
  • Lifetime buy-back on every piece.* Insured delivery across Europe.
III

Conscientious

con·sci·en·tious · adj. · Careful to do what is right, in every detail.

A laboratory-grown diamond has a known origin and no link to conflict. Each ASHTH diamond is certified and each piece is hallmarked, so you know exactly what you are wearing, where it came from and what it is made of.

  • The proof
  • Every diamond independently certified; every piece hallmarked.
  • No association with conflict sourcing at any point in the chain.
  • Full transparency on metal purity and carat weight, in writing.

Three words. One standard. A Sustainable, Sensible, Conscientious Choice.

Our Signature · Patented

The ASHTH Cut: 97 facets, eight sides, one signature.

Our signature diamond is an octagon of 97 facets, cut exclusively for ASHTH. Seen from above, it reveals a pattern of arrows. Seen from below, a ring of hearts. Its eight sides echo the eight facets in our name, and every facet is placed to draw out the greatest brilliance and fire.

ASHTH takes its name from an ancient word for the eight facets of a diamond. Each facet of our mark carries one of the eight values we hold ourselves to, in every stone and every setting.
